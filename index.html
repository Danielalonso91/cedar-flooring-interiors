About Cedar

A smaller contractor experience—with more personal attention.

Cedar Home Services & Interiors was built around a simple idea: homeowners should be able to hire someone for the projects that fall between a quick repair and a full remodel without sacrificing workmanship or professionalism.

We focus on manageable, high-value home projects and take pride in thoughtful planning, careful installation, clean work areas, and communication you don’t have to chase down.
