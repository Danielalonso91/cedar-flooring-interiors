CEDAR FLOORING & INTERIORS WEBSITE

How to use:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in your browser to preview.
3. Replace placeholder images inside assets/images/placeholders or add your real photos to assets/images/portfolio.
4. In index.html, update the image src paths to match your real image filenames.
5. Update phone, email, and service area in the Contact section.
6. Deploy on Netlify Drop by dragging the whole cedar-flooring-interiors folder into Netlify.

Netlify contact form:
The form is already configured with data-netlify="true" and a hidden form-name input.
After deploying on Netlify, submissions should appear inside your Netlify dashboard under Forms.

Best image sizes:
- Hero image: 1600x1200 or larger
- Gallery images: 1200x900 works well
- Use compressed JPG/WebP files for faster load speed.
