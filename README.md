# Cedar Home Services & Interiors

Static Netlify-ready website adapted from the Cedar Flooring & Interiors concept.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `YOUR-EMAIL@example.com` and `(000) 000-0000` in `index.html`.
2. Replace the three project placeholders with your real before/after or finished-project photos.
3. If you want to keep the original business name, replace `Cedar Home Services & Interiors` with `Cedar Flooring & Interiors` throughout `index.html`.
4. Review any service descriptions against the work you actually intend to offer and any local licensing requirements.

## Netlify
The quote form is already configured with `data-netlify="true"`. Deploy the folder through your existing GitHub → Netlify workflow or drag the folder into a Netlify site.

## Design intent
- Preserves the premium craftsman positioning of the earlier flooring site.
- Keeps flooring as the flagship service.
- Adds trim, doors, bathroom upgrades, accent walls/shelving, and bundled punch-list handyman work.
- Avoids positioning Cedar as a cheap hourly handyman service.
- Emphasizes service visits, half-day work, and Saturday-sized projects.
